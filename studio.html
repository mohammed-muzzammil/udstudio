The studio

Design that feels lived in

UD Studio is an interior design studio in Shantinagar, Nagpur. We design complete homes, from living rooms and bedrooms to kitchens and wardrobes, as well as offices, cafés and restaurants.

Founder

Light first

Before we pick a single colour, we study where the sun comes in and how each room is used at night. Good light makes a modest room feel generous.

Made for living

Homes are for families, guests, celebrations and everyday mess. We design storage, surfaces and finishes that still look good after years of real life.

Every detail considered

Handles, lighting, edges and finishes: the small things you touch every day get the same attention as the big picture.

How we work

Five steps to a finished home

  1. Listen & measure

    A first meeting at the studio or your site. We learn how you live, take measurements and agree on the scope.

  2. Plan the layout

    Space plans and furniture layouts, so movement, storage and light work before anything is built.

  3. Design & materials

    Finishes, colours and fabrics, chosen with you and checked in daylight and evening light.

  4. Build it right

    The work happens on site, following the design you approved.

  5. Welcome home

    The finishing touches go in, the lights come on, and you move in.
